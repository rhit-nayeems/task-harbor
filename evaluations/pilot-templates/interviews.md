## Recent work

1. Show the last maintenance task you completed with an agent.
2. What did you do before the agent started? What information was missing?
3. Where did you intervene, and why?
4. How did you decide the change was ready to merge?
5. What went wrong? How much time did recovery take?
6. Which tasks do you avoid giving to agents?

Record observed behavior separately from the developer's estimates.

## First Harbor task

- Could they complete the demo without help?
- Could they configure their own repository and checks?
- Where did they stop or ask for help?
- Did they understand the review evidence and remaining uncertainty?
- Would they choose Harbor for their next real task? Which task?

## Follow-up

- Did they return without a reminder? Record actual runs, not intentions.
- What did they continue doing directly with their agent?
- What would stop them using Harbor next week?
- What currently costs the team time or money that Harbor could remove?
- If offered continued use at a stated price, what did they do?

Interest, a verbal commitment, and payment are different outcomes. Record the
actual outcome, including rejections. Do not suggest hypothetical savings as facts.
